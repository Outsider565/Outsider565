## Research

I study the science of large-language-model pre-training: how architectures,
optimization, and data distributions shape efficiency, scaling behavior,
knowledge acquisition, hallucination, and long-context generalization.

Current themes include:

- compute-efficient architectures and scaling laws;
- optimization and parameterization for stable model scaling;
- long-context modeling and positional representations;
- understanding model behavior through compression and data distributions.

For my full research profile, publications, and current biography, please
visit **[my personal website](https://outsider565.github.io/)**.
